01 What this paper does

The authors build a stepwise, small-molecule + growth-factor recipe that pushes human pluripotent stem cells (hPSCs) down the kidney lineage — through mesendoderm → intermediate mesoderm (IM) → either proximal-tubule-like tubules or cap-mesenchyme nephron progenitors — entirely in defined monolayer culture, with no cell sorting. This report extracts everything you asked for: the full treatment catalog, every combination they screened, the timing & duration of each step, and a stage-by-stage marker catalog.

The one-sentence protocol

Treat hPSCs with the GSK-3β inhibitor CHIR99021 to make mesendoderm (~100% BRACHYURY⁺), then add FGF2 + retinoic acid to make PAX2⁺LHX1⁺ IM by day 3. Withdraw factors → ciliated tubules expressing proximal-tubule markers. Or add FGF9 + activin A → SIX2⁺SALL1⁺WT1⁺ cap-mesenchyme nephron progenitors.

Why it mattered

  • First demonstration that FGF signaling (FGF2) potently induces IM-specific PAX2 in hPSCs.
  • First generation of SIX2⁺ nephron-progenitor cells from hPSCs.
  • Fastest/most efficient IM protocol of its time — 3 days, 70–80%, no FACS — vs. 11–18 days elsewhere.

Duration rules that change fate:
  • CHIR 24 h then withdraw → 30–40% revert to OCT4⁺ (incompletely differentiated).
  • CHIR 48 h → eliminates OCT4⁺; >80% FOXF1⁺ (lateral plate). Longer CHIR pushes lateral plate fate.
  • CHIR 36 h is the sweet spot for downstream IM (highest PAX2).
Transience to respect:
  • Primitive-streak genes peak 36–48 h, gone by 72 h.
  • IM (PAX2) peaks day 3, falls to <10% by day 7 — capture/branch promptly.
  • Definitive-endoderm window: activin works at 24 h, fails at 48 h.

08 Key findings & context

1 · FGF is the missing IM signal

FGF2 is identified for the first time as a potent inducer of IM-specific PAX2 in CHIR-primed hPSCs — adding FGF signaling to the previously known Wnt/activin/BMP/RA toolkit.

2 · Fast & sort-free

PAX2⁺LHX1⁺ IM in just 3 days at 70–80% efficiency, reproducible across 5 hPSC lines without FACS — faster than the 11–18 day OSR1-GFP approach of Mae et al.

3 · First hPSC-derived SIX2⁺ progenitors

FGF9 + activin A yields SIX2⁺SALL1⁺WT1⁺ cap-mesenchyme cells, consistent with FGF9's in-vivo role maintaining nephron progenitors.

4 · BMP7 does NOT help

Contrary to several earlier protocols, adding BMP7 to FGF2+RA reduced PAX2 — a notable negative result.

5 · Timing dictates fate

Identical factors give different outcomes by timing: CHIR length tunes IM vs. lateral plate; activin at 24 h (not 48 h) gives endoderm; IM is a transient 2–3 day window.

6 · Functional validation

Tubules are polarized & ciliated; day-9 cells integrate into mouse metanephric explants and form AQP1⁺ growths under the kidney capsule — though integration is incomplete.

Contrast with the literature: differs from Tan et al. (prolonged GSK-3β inhibition → endoderm; here endoderm needs CHIR + high activin) and improves on Mae et al. (OSR1-GFP marks both IM and lateral plate, lower PAX2/WT1 co-expression, longer protocol).
