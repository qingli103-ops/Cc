01 What this paper does

This is the direct successor to Lam et al. (JASN 2014). Where the earlier paper reached intermediate mesoderm (PAX2⁺LHX1⁺) and early tubules, this one builds a complete, chemically defined route from hPSCs to multipotent nephron progenitor cells (NPCs) at 90% efficiency in 9 days, then lets those NPCs self-organize into segmented nephron-like structures — glomerular podocytes, proximal tubules, loops of Henle, and distal tubules — in both 2D and 3D, and uses the organoids to model kidney development and toxicity.

① Efficient NPCs

CHIR → activin → FGF9 yields SIX2⁺SALL1⁺WT1⁺PAX2⁺ NPCs at ~90% in 9 days — a fully defined 2D monolayer protocol.

② Self-organizing nephrons

A transient CHIR pulse drives NPCs into PAX8⁺LHX1⁺ renal vesicles that self-pattern by day 21 into contiguous, segmented nephron structures.

③ A working model

Organoids reproduce Notch-dependent proximal patterning and report drug injury (gentamicin, cisplatin) via KIM-1 and γH2AX.

Why it's relevant to you: this is the protocol your Stage 1–2 work is built on — CHIR 8 µM × 4 d is exactly its primitive-streak step — and its terminal cell types are your readouts: NPHS1⁺PODXL⁺ podocytes = iPODO and LTL⁺ proximal tubules = iPTEC. See How it connects.

02 The big idea: posterior vs anterior intermediate mesoderm

The conceptual leap that makes the efficiency jump possible. Metanephric NPCs (the source of all nephron epithelia except collecting duct) arise specifically from posterior IM. Earlier protocols mis-targeted this step.

The mistake earlier protocols made

  • They pushed cells to the posterior primitive streak using BMP4, assuming that's the kidney route.
  • But posterior primitive streak → lateral plate mesoderm (FOXF1⁺), not IM.
  • Result: low efficiency and mixed identities.

The fix here

  • Posterior IM comes from the late-stage (mid) primitive streak, not the posterior streak.
  • Tune two variables: A–P position (CHIR dose + suppress BMP4 with Noggin) and migration timing (CHIR duration = 4 days).
  • Read it out correctly: posterior IM = WT1⁺HOXD11⁺, and crucially PAX2⁻ LHX1⁻ (those mark anterior IM).
Sensitivity to BMP4: CHIR can drive endogenous BMP4, posteriorizing cells to FOXF1⁺ lateral plate. Adding BMP4 (1 ng/mL) reproduces the failure; adding Noggin (5 ng/mL) rescues it. Each cell line needs its BMP4 tone tuned — the main hESC↔hiPSC difference in the protocol.

05 2D nephrons vs 3D organoids

2D monolayer

Renal vesicles self-pattern into elongated, segmented nephron structures by day 21; nephron-like structures formed >20× more efficiently than the previous (Lam 2014) protocol and persisted to day 56.

3D suspension organoids

Dissociate day-9 NPCs into round-bottom ultra-low-attachment wells (1×10⁵/well) → one organoid per well. Day-9 replating gave the best induction. EM showed foot processes, Bowman's-capsule-like encapsulation, tubular lumens, tight junctions, brush borders, mitochondria-rich PT cells.

What did not help boosting LTL⁺ tubules in 3D: co-culture with Wnt4-NIH3T3 or a ureteric-bud line, a 6-factor cocktail (BMP7, HGF, IGF-1, JAG1, WNT4, Wnt9b), or CHIR/IWR-1/SB431542 — pointing instead to the importance of correct temporal signaling already built into the protocol.

07 Modeling applications

Kidney development — Notch patterning

Adding the Notch inhibitor DAPT (10 µM, days 14–21) markedly suppressed proximal-tubule formation in 2D and 3D — recapitulating Notch's known role in proximal nephron patterning. The organoid is a working developmental model.

Nephrotoxicity — drug injury

  • Gentamicin (5 mg/mL, 48 h): KIM-1⁺ at the luminal surface of LTL⁺ proximal tubules (dose-dependent by qPCR); E-cadherin⁺ distal tubules spared.
  • Cisplatin (5 µM, 24 h): KIM-1↑ in PT and E-cadherin suppressed (proximal + distal toxicity).
  • γH2AX: 5 µM cisplatin → DNA damage in LTL⁺ PT only; 50 µM → widespread (generalized toxicity).
Because the organoids carry the major nephron segments, they enable segment-specific, patient-specific (iPSC) nephrotoxicity screening — a use case directly relevant to drug development.

08 Advances over prior work & limitations

Advances

  • 90% SIX2⁺ NPCs in 9 days — vs ~20% in prior hPSC protocols.
  • Nephron-like structures >20× more efficient than the previous (Lam 2014) protocol.
  • Posterior-IM strategy (late streak, BMP4 control) instead of posterior streak → correct lineage.
  • Very low FGF9 (10 ng/mL) — ~20× less than earlier reports — implying properly primed precursors.
  • Fully chemically defined, 2D monolayer, fewer steps, no mouse embryonic spinal cord.
  • Contiguous, segmented, mature nephrons (lumens, foot processes, brush borders); no collecting-duct/ureteric-bud contamination.

Limitations (by design)

  • No ureteric bud → no collecting ducts.
  • No vascular progenitors → no glomerular capillary tuft.
  • Interstitial stroma is minimal (FOXD1⁻).
  • Nephrons self-organize without these components — useful, but not a whole kidney.

09 How it connects — to Lam 2014 and to your protocol

vs Lam et al. 2014 (the predecessor)

  • Lam: hPSC → anterior IM (PAX2⁺LHX1⁺) via CHIR → FGF2 + RA; reached early tubules & first SIX2⁺ cells.
  • Morizane: targets posterior IM (WT1⁺HOXD11⁺) via CHIR → activin → FGF9; reaches 90% NPCs and full segmented nephrons.
  • Same lab, same lineage logic (PSC→ME/PS→IM→MM), but a sharper A–P targeting and a self-organizing organoid endpoint.

vs your Stage 1–2 work

  • Your Stage 1 (CHIR 8 µM × 4 d) is exactly this paper's late-primitive-streak step.
  • Your readouts map onto its terminal cells: iPODO ↔ NPHS1⁺PODXL⁺ podocytes; iPTEC (LTL⁺) ↔ LTL⁺ proximal tubules.
  • It hands you defined windows + markers for the steps you wanted to pin down: posterior-IM checkpoint (WT1⁺HOXD11⁺, PAX2⁻LHX1⁻ at D7) and the NPC checkpoint (SIX2⁺SALL1⁺WT1⁺PAX2⁺ at D9).
Practical takeaways for your "minimal-cue at the right window" goal: activin (D4–7) is the lever that decides posterior IM, and a transient low CHIR (3 µM, D9–11) is the lever that converts NPCs to renal vesicles. Reading WT1/HOXD11 at D7 and SIX2 at D9 would tell you whether your auto-pilot is hitting these checkpoints — and a low FGF9 (10 ng/mL) at D7 is the documented cue to lift NPC (hence downstream iPTEC) yield.
